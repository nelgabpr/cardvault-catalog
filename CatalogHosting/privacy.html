CardVault AI Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026

CardVault AI is designed as an offline-first collection tool. Card scans and recognition are processed on the device.

Data stored by the app

The app stores collection records, wishlist records, notes, purchase information, card images, and cached market estimates on the device using Apple SwiftData.

Network activity

CardVault AI connects to the internet to download validated catalog updates, card artwork, and catalog-supplied market estimates. Captured card photographs are not sent to the catalog service or a recognition server.

Tracking and advertising

CardVault AI does not include advertising SDKs and does not track users across apps or websites. The current release does not collect developer analytics events.

Backups and deletion

Users may export a JSON vault backup. Exported files are controlled by the user and may contain collection details and card images. Individual entries can be deleted inside the app. Deleting the app removes local data; exported backups must be deleted separately.

Contact

Support is available through the CardVault AI support page.
