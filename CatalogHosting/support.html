CardVault AI Support

Before contacting support

Open Settings in CardVault AI and note the app version and catalog version. For scanner issues, record the card name, set, collector number, lighting, and whether the card was sleeved.

Common fixes

  • Camera unavailable: enable Camera access in iPhone Settings.
  • Card not recognized: remove glare, fill the guide with the card, or use manual capture.
  • Wrong printing: choose another candidate using the set and collector number.
  • Missing price: confirm the exact printing, condition, and finish; some cards do not have a current market estimate.
  • Missing collection: import a previously exported vault backup from Files or iCloud Drive.
  • App no longer available: reconnect the iPhone to the development Mac and reinstall, or use a TestFlight/App Store build after distribution is enabled.

Include with a report

In CardVault AI, open Settings → Support Diagnostics → Copy Diagnostics. The report excludes card names, notes, images, purchase prices, and API keys. For recognition problems, also include the expected card name, set, and collector number.

Contact

A monitored support email must be added before public App Store release. During TestFlight, submit feedback through the TestFlight app.
